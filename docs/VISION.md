# Vision

My AI Video Studio is a **free-first, local-first AI film engine**: a short idea goes in, an upload-ready short film comes out —
anime action, superhuman stories, documentaries, cinematic shorts or episodic series — with every moving image produced by
a generative video model, and every production saved as a portable project that can be re-edited or continued as the next episode.

Principles
1. **Generated, not assembled.** Visuals come from video diffusion models. Compositing and editing support them; stock footage never replaces them.
2. **Free by default.** Open weights, local inference, free-tier APIs only as optional accelerators. No mandatory paid service.
3. **Honest output.** Every video ships with a generation report: models, reasons, native vs export resolution, limitations, cost.
4. **Memory.** Characters, worlds and episodes persist (`.studio.zip`), so a series can continue across sessions and machines.
5. **Hardware-aware.** The same pipeline runs on 4 CPU cores (baseline) and picks better models when a GPU exists.
