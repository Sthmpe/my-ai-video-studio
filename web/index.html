How a film is made

Every stage is resumable and quality-gated.

01

Story & cast

Hook-first script, dialogue, original characters with reference sheets and fixed voices.

02

Keyframes

Each shot gets a full-quality anime keyframe, guided by the character's reference via IP-Adapter.

03

Animation

AnimateDiff-Lightning animates the keyframe (image-to-video) with camera-motion LoRAs; bad clips are re-seeded.

04

Sound & edit

Kokoro EN/JA voices, scene-timed SFX and ambience, original score, subtitles, Whisper-checked speech.
